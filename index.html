PixelAir Audio Bridge

Send the audio playing on your Mac to Fluora plants on your local network.

Download PixelAir Audio Bridge 0.1.1

macOS 14 or later · Developer ID signed and notarized
Open the disk image and drag the app into Applications.

Highlights

  • Listens to a virtual audio device such as BlackHole or Loopback.
  • Turns the sound into 128 frequency bands on the Mac.
  • Assigns each plant the mix, the left channel, or the right channel.
  • Saves presets for the input, levels, bin maps, and plant assignments.

Tutorial

Tutorial video

Choose the input

Point the app at the loopback device that is receiving your system audio. PixelAir Audio Bridge does not appear inside Loopback as a monitor. You select that device here, then choose whether plants update at 50 or 100 times a second.

Shape the level

Gain, range, attack, release, and a low-to-high bias sit next to a live EQ visualizer. Switch the view between left, right, and mix, and preview a bin map before a plant uses it.

Send each plant its own part

Fluora plants on the local network show up in the device list. Set one to the full mix, or split a stereo pair so one plant follows the left channel and another follows the right. You can scan again or add a plant by IP.

Zoom in on a frequency range

A bin map takes a slice of the spectrum, such as a kick or a snare, and stretches it across all 128 bins. Name a few maps, save the whole setup as a preset, and switch between shows without rebuilding the routing.
