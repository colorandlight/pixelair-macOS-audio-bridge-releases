PixelAir Audio Bridge

Send the audio playing on your Mac to Fluora plants on your local network.

  1. Capture Listens to a virtual audio device such as BlackHole or Loopback.
  2. Analyze Splits the sound into 128 frequency bands on the Mac.
  3. Stream Each plant can follow the mix, the left channel, or the right channel.
Download PixelAir Audio Bridge 0.1.1

macOS 14 or later · Developer ID signed and notarized

Install

  1. Open the disk image and drag PixelAir Audio Bridge into Applications.
  2. Route your Mac’s audio into BlackHole or Loopback.
  3. Choose that input, assign each plant Mix, Left, or Right, and turn Streaming on.

The app checks for updates in the background and asks before installing. Leave it running during a live stream.
